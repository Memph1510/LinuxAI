---
title: Check out a broker before booking
use: freight/broker-check
model: claude
tags: research, credit, reputation
---
Research the freight broker {{broker}} (MC number {{mc_number}} if known).
Find, from credible sources: authority status and age, bond, credit or days-to-pay ratings, recent complaints,
double-brokering or fraud reports, and how they treat carriers.
Rate each source you use (trusted / useful / doubtful). End with: book with confidence, book with caution
(and what to watch for), or avoid, and why. Say what you couldn't verify.
