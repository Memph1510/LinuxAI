Make {{subject}} for {{purpose}}. Before generating anything, set up the scene: what's in it and where (foreground,
middle, background), the camera (height, angle, lens: wide or tight), the light (direction, softness, time of day),
and the pose or arrangement of the main subject. When the composition has to be exact (a product shot, a layout,
a pose), block it out first in Blender (simple shapes, the camera and lights) and render a rough preview; then
generate from that preview as a reference, so the picture follows the layout instead of chance. Check the result
against the setup: say what matches and what doesn't.
